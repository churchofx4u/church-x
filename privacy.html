Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026 · Last updated October 13, 2026

The short version: Worship With Me keeps your church's songs, services, and visitor cards on your own computer, inside your browser. We don't run a server that stores them, and we don't sell data. The few exceptions are listed below.

Who we are

Worship With Me is made by Church X ("we", "us"). Contact: churchofx4u@gmail.com.

What stays on your computer

Everything below is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use. We cannot see any of it.

  • Your church name, settings, slide backgrounds, and the public URL you enter.
  • Services, run sheets, team roles, and stage notes.
  • Songs, including licensed lyrics you paste in and their CCLI or OneLicense numbers.
  • The CCLI log of songs shown.
  • "New here" visitor cards: name, phone, first-time or returning, an optional prayer or note, the time, and whether someone followed up.

If you clear your browser data, this data is deleted. Use Export backup to keep a copy. Files you export (Sunday files, backups, the visitor CSV) are yours to store and share. Please protect them, especially the visitor CSV.

What can leave your computer

  • Usage notice (only if you agree). The first time you press Present, the app asks whether it may send us a short note. If you say yes, at most once a day it sends your church name, the service name, the date, and the app version to us through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which emails it to churchofx4u@gmail.com. It does not include songs, lyrics, people, or visitor cards. You can say no, or change your answer later on the Church screen.
  • QR code. To draw the "New here" QR code, the app sends the welcome page address (not any visitor data) to a QR service, api.qrserver.com.
  • YouTube. If you add a YouTube video, it plays in an embedded YouTube player. YouTube (Google) may collect data under its own privacy policy, including cookies.
  • Hosting. The app is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when the page loads, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Email. If you contact us or use "Email this note", your email app sends what you write. We use it only to answer you.

Visitor cards: the church's responsibility

When a church uses the "New here" card, the church collects and holds that information, not us. The church decides how to follow up and should tell visitors how their information will be used. We never receive visitor cards.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking scripts of our own.
  • We don't sell or rent any information.

Children

The app is meant for church staff and volunteers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Churches should not record children on visitor cards without a parent's consent.

Payments

Payment is not turned on yet. When it is, a payment provider will handle card details, and we will update this policy before it starts.

Your choices and rights

You control the data on your computer: edit it, export it, or delete it by clearing your browser data. To ask about or delete usage notices we received from you, email churchofx4u@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Big changes, like adding accounts or payments, will be shown in the app.
