How to use this

  1. Gather the facts. Send the full name, the birth date, the death date, the day and hour, the place, and the order of the service. Send one clear photo of the person, with the face easy to see.
  2. Message @realchurchx on X with those facts and the photo. Do not send a card number.
  3. Pay $25 with X Money. Open X, then Money, then Send, and pay @realchurchx. On the web, Send is in the menu at the top right of a post. Enter 25. You need an X Money account. The transfer is instant. There is no card checkout on this page.
  4. One PDF comes back within 24 hours after the $25 shows. Page one is the bulletin, with the photo. Page two is the run sheet for the people leading the service.
  5. The sample below shows the layout. Click Add a photo to see where the picture sits. That try stays on this computer. It is not the order, and it is not uploaded.
