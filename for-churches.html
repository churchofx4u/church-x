WORSHIP WITH ME Sunday slides for your church

For the church laptop

Lyrics on the projector. The run sheet on the laptop.

Worship With Me is a Sunday program for one church. The songs go on the screen. The order, the team, and the notes stay with the person running the service.

Open the app Message @realchurchx

What a church gets

  • Present opens a projector window. This laptop keeps the run sheet.
  • Public-domain hymns are built in. Licensed lyrics stay on the church computer.
  • The church name is the title slide. A licensed song ends with the author and license number.
  • Export Sunday, take the file to the building, and import it there.
  • If the wifi drops after the first open, it still opens.
